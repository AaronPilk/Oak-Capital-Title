Commercial
Commercial Title Insurance

Coverage that matches the deal

Commercial property carries a bigger price tag and a purchase agreement with far more in it. Entities, assignments, leases, easements, mechanic's liens from work nobody remembers authorizing.

The search has to go deeper and the policy has to be sized to what is genuinely at risk. That is the difference between a commercial file and a residential one, and it is why they do not get run the same way.

Every real estate transaction benefits from it. Commercial deals need it more, simply because the purchase prices are higher and the agreements behind them are more complicated — which means there is more to lose if the chain of title turns out to have a hole in it.

Anything that goes wrong with the property's title after you own it. Sometimes that is a clerical error in a recorded document. Sometimes it is negligence by a previous party. Occasionally it is outright fraud.

Without a policy, an investor who loses a title dispute has no recourse and no way to recover the investment.

There is a lender's policy and an owner's policy. If you are borrowing, you are buying both — the lender's protects them, the owner's protects you.

The owner's policy stays in force for as long as you hold the property. One premium, no renewals.

Send us the contract. On a commercial file, the agency you pick matters more than on a house — the search is deeper and the underwriting judgment calls are real. Two decades of these is what we bring to it.
